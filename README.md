## Hi, I'm Sean

I'm a senior product designer working where design meets engineering. Right now I design streaming experiences for HBO Max at Warner Bros. Discovery, and before that I worked on Fire TV at Amazon. Along the way I've found that the fastest way to settle a design question is often to build the thing. So I design in Figma, and when a static mock can't show the idea, I prototype it in code.

This account is where I keep the things I've designed and built myself, under my multimedia venture, Combinator Media.

### What's here

**[Monocle](https://github.com/CombinatorMedia/monocle)**: a Chrome extension I designed and shipped that turns Google Images into a fullscreen viewer, with filmstrip navigation, a shortlist, and side-by-side compare. The repo includes a [design-history](https://github.com/CombinatorMedia/monocle/tree/main/design-history) folder that walks through each iteration, from the first AI-generated draft to the version on the Chrome Web Store, and there's a [demo video](https://youtu.be/LlKX2AwHkBQ) of it running in real time.

**[Prototypes](https://github.com/CombinatorMedia/prototypes)**: interactive prototypes from my product design work, each published with its own link so it can be clicked through rather than just looked at.

### How I work

I lean on AI tools throughout my process, from early concept exploration to writing production code with Claude as a collaborator, and I treat them the way I treat Figma: a tool I'm responsible for directing, auditioning, and polishing until the result is mine.
